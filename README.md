# Excel Assignment 1 – Data Exploration

## Objective
To explore and analyze product data using basic Excel formulas and functions.

## Method Used

**SUM** – To calculate the total price of all products.
**COUNTA** – To count the total number of products.
**AVERAGE** – To calculate the average product price.
**MIN** – To identify the minimum product price.
**MAX** – To identify the maximum product price.
**IF** – To classify products into "High Price" and "Standard Price" categories.
**SUMIF** – To calculate the total price of products belonging to the Electronics category.
**COUNTIF** – To count products with a price below $100.
**LEFT** – To extract the day from the date.
**RIGHT** – To extract the country code.
**MID** – To extract the month from the date.
